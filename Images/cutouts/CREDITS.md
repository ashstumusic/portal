# Cutout star sheet (`stars.webp`)

The paper stars scattered over the homepage are real engraved stars, cut
out of plates from Johann Bayer's *Uranometria* (1603, engraved by
Alexander Mair), via Wikimedia Commons: thresholded to stark black ink,
printed onto aged paper taken from the plates' own blank areas, and cut
out with rough scissor edges. Made by `scripts/make_star_cutouts.py`.
